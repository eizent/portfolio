## Digital Projects

**Modeling a Divided Berlin: Parallel Lives Across Multilingual Cities**, a digital map experience ([https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/e413d93e80e0409ca2525c6ce3f1e7e9](https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/e413d93e80e0409ca2525c6ce3f1e7e9){:target="_blank"}

*Role*: Project Manager, GIS Lead, Fabrication Manager  

*About the Project*: The goal of this site is to create a digital representation of divided Berlin of the 1980s to be used in language-learning courses in Russian and German at Bryn Mawr College. This website focuses on gathering archival materials and building a web-based map that includes images of buildings, landmarks, and daily life, along with information in three languages: Russian, German, and English. Throughout this project, I worked closely with Professor Irina Walsh (Russian department) and Professor Margaret Strair (German department) to understand their needs and customize both the digital map and the physical map to align with their vision of use within the classroom.

**Philadelphia the Global City: The Italian Legacy across Time**, a digital map and collection of photos and essays ([digbmc.github.io/philly-global-city](https://digbmc.github.io/philly-global-city/){:target="_blank"})  

*Role*: Co-Project Manager, Web Development, Design, and Implementation  

*About the Project*: This goal of this project was to create a digital collection of locations in Philadelphia tied to the legacy of transnational Italian culture researched by students in the Fall 2025 course 'ITAL B240 Philadelphia the Global City: The Italian Legacy across Time'. The website features an interactive map of key locations, artworks, and other points of interest related to the Italian legacy in Philadelphia along with student research on each point of interest. 

**Paul Thomas Annotated: In the Margins** ([ptannotated.com](https://ptannotated.com){:target="_blank"})

*Role*: Web Map Developer

*About the Project*: Paul Thomas Anderson: in the Margins is an open access resource devoted to the filmography of Paul Thomas Anderson, involving annotated screenshots of the director's 10 films. My contribution to the project was compiling and organizing geographic data of filming locations as well as creating a Map Feature for the site. I customized the Leaflet.js web map to suit the project needs, including adding a functional legend, adjusting the pop-ups, and adapting the structure of the CollectionBuilder site to include the map data as a secondary collection to the primary collection of screenshots.
